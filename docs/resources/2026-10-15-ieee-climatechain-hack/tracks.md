# Tracks — IEEE ClimateChain Global Hackathon

> Fetched 2026-10-15. Primary: Devpost overview "What to Build" (https://ieee-climatechain-hack.devpost.com/). Secondary-but-organizer: IEEE Blockchain Türkiye page (https://cmte.ieee.org/turkiye-blockchain/hackatlon/), which names and describes the tracks **differently** (kept separately below). Tracks are **absent from the Devpost rules page** (conflict (c)).
> Overview framing (verbatim): `Build solutions that address real-world climate challenges using blockchain, AI, and data-driven approaches, aligned with ClimateChain tracks. Your project should belong to one of the defined tracks.`
> Per-track prize: none — prizes are overall (1st/2nd/3rd), not per track. Minimum bar to qualify: only "Track Alignment: Clearly state the track you've chosen and how your project addresses the challenge." Required tech: **none mandated** on Devpost. No sponsor SDK/bounty tracks.

## Devpost version (verbatim table row content)

### 1. Carbon Markets & Emissions Transparency
- **Challenge:** Improve trust, transparency, and efficiency in carbon markets
- **Problem:** Lack of transparency, double counting, and complex verification reduce credibility
- **Build (example directions):**
  - Blockchain-based carbon credit verification
  - Real-time emissions tracking
  - Smart contracts to prevent double counting
  - Transparent carbon trading platforms

### 2. Renewable Energy & Energy Trading
- **Challenge:** Enable decentralized and efficient energy systems
- **Problem:** Energy markets remain centralized and inefficient despite renewable growth
- **Build:**
  - Peer-to-peer energy trading
  - Renewable certificate tracking
  - Microgrid coordination systems
  - Tokenized incentives for clean energy

### 3. Sustainable Supply Chains
- **Challenge:** Increase transparency and accountability in supply chains
- **Problem:** Difficult to verify ethical sourcing and sustainability claims
- **Build:**
  - Product lifecycle tracking
  - Digital product passports
  - Recycling traceability systems
  - Circular economy incentive platforms

### 4. Climate Data & Environmental Monitoring
- **Challenge:** Enable reliable and accessible climate data systems
- **Problem:** Environmental data is fragmented and difficult to verify
- **Build:**
  - Climate data verification platforms
  - IoT environmental monitoring
  - Climate data marketplaces
  - Tools for research and insights

## Organizer-page version (IEEE Blockchain Türkiye Group) — differently named, different examples

Intro (verbatim): `Based on climate action priorities and UN COP 31 focus areas, we prepared 4 tracks:`

- **Track 1: Carbon Markets & Emissions Trading** — Transparent carbon credit verification and trading platforms; Decentralized carbon offset marketplaces; Real-time emissions tracking and reporting systems; Smart contracts for carbon credit retirement and compliance
- **Track 2: Renewable Energy & Grid Management** — Peer-to-peer renewable energy trading platforms; Decentralized energy grid coordination; Renewable energy certificate (REC) tracking and verification; Microgrid management and energy storage optimization
- **Track 3: Supply Chain Transparency & Circular Economy** — Product lifecycle tracking for sustainability verification; Recycling and waste management traceability systems; Sustainable sourcing and ethical supply chain verification; Circular economy token systems and incentive structures
- **Track 4: Climate Data & Environmental Monitoring** — Decentralized climate data collection and verification networks; IoT sensor networks for air quality and environmental monitoring; Transparent climate research data sharing platforms; Satellite data integration for deforestation and land use tracking
