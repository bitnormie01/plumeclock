# IEEE Blockchain Türkiye Group (lead organizer)

Role (Devpost overview, verbatim): `IEEE Blockchain Türkiye Group (https://cmte.ieee.org/turkiye-blockchain) organizes this hackhaton in collaboration in collaboration with IEEE Blockchain Technical Group, IEEE Orange County EMBS, IEEE Orange County Section, IEEE Orange County Chapter, IEEE Türkiye Section, The Istanbul Chamber of Commerce, AI Ecosystem Association of Türkiye.`

What it cares about (from its own site, https://cmte.ieee.org/turkiye-blockchain , fetched 2026-10-15): supporting, collaborating and coordinating blockchain activities in Türkiye — `A group from Turkish universities and industries now was established and approved by the IEEE Blockchain and the IEEE Türkiye Section to support, collaborate and coordinate blockchain activities in Türkiye.` Its page for this event (https://cmte.ieee.org/turkiye-blockchain/hackatlon/) frames the hackathon as: `an online hackathon in the topics of climate change, emission reduction, and promoting global sustainability`; goal `leverage blockchain technology to address critical climate change challenges and support the goals of UN COP 31`; emphasis on `climate data transparency, carbon markets, and sustainable resource management` and `enhance environmental accountability with blockchain-enabled tools`. (That page says "blockchain technology" only; Devpost adds AI — conflict (p).)

People (Devpost overview): Dr. Güven Fidan — Hackathon Team Leader / Co-Chair; Prof. Dr. Şeref Sağıroğlu — Chair; Prof. Dr. Sedat Akleylek — Co-Chair; Dr. Elvan Duman — Youth Chair; Dr. Kübra Seyhan — Women Chair; Efehan Maleri — Community Building Chair. Chapter site lists Sağıroğlu (Gazi University), Fidan (MindMons Technologies), Akleylek (University of Tartu). Most of these are also Devpost judges.
